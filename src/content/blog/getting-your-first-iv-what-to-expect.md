Booking your first IV can feel like a step into the unknown. The good news is that the process is simple, comfortable, and designed around you. Here is exactly what to expect from your first mobile IV session with IV Salt Rejuvenation, from the moment you book to the moment we pack up and leave you feeling refreshed.

## Step 1: Booking and confirming

It all starts online. You choose your IV therapy, add any boosts you would like, and pick a preferred time. We follow up to confirm the details.

Before your appointment is locked in, you will complete a short medical consent form and place a refundable deposit that applies to your treatment. This helps us prepare the right infusion for you and reserve your time slot.

## Step 2: We come to you

One of the best parts of mobile IV therapy is that you never have to leave home. A licensed Registered Nurse arrives at your chosen location with everything needed for a clean, safe, and relaxing session.

Pick a spot where you can sit back comfortably, whether that is your couch, a favorite chair, or a spot by the pool. There is no waiting room and no rushing.

## Step 3: Your quick health check

Your nurse will review your medical screening, answer any questions, and take a moment to check in on how you are feeling that day. This is a great time to mention anything relevant, such as allergies, medications, or how well you slept and hydrated.

This short conversation helps your nurse tailor the experience and make sure IV therapy is a good fit for you.

## Step 4: Placing the IV

Next comes the part most people wonder about. Your nurse will clean the area, usually on your arm or hand, and place a small, thin catheter into a vein. You may feel a brief pinch, similar to a standard blood draw. After that, the needle is removed and only a soft, flexible tube stays in place, so you can relax and even use your phone.

If you tend to feel nervous about needles, let your nurse know. Simple things like taking a slow breath, looking away, and staying warm and hydrated can make a big difference.

## Step 5: Relax and rehydrate

Once your IV is running, the rest is easy. Fluids, vitamins, and nutrients flow directly into your bloodstream for full absorption. Most infusions take about 45 to 60 minutes, while NAD+ therapy is infused more slowly and takes longer.

Bring a book, put on a show, or simply close your eyes. Many clients say this is the most relaxing hour of their week.

## Step 6: Aftercare

When your infusion is complete, your nurse removes the catheter, applies a small bandage, and makes sure you feel good before leaving. There is very little downtime. You can usually return to your normal day right away.

To get the most from your session, keep drinking water, eat a nourishing meal, and listen to your body. Everyone responds a little differently, and results can vary from person to person.

## A few friendly tips for first timers

- Eat a light meal beforehand so you feel your best during the session.
- Drink water in the hours leading up to your appointment.
- Wear comfortable clothing with easy access to your arm.
- Write down any questions so you remember to ask your nurse.
- Give yourself permission to fully relax. This is your time.

Your first IV is the start of a simple, feel-good wellness habit. Whether you are recovering, staying ahead of a busy season, or simply investing in yourself, we are here to make it easy and enjoyable.
