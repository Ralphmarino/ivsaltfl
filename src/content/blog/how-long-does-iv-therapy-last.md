Two questions come up before almost every first booking: how long does IV therapy last, and does it really do anything? Both deserve straight answers, so here they are, from the team at [IV Salt Rejuvenation](/), where every drip is administered by a licensed registered nurse under medical director supervision.

  <p class="qa-label">Quick answer</p>

Let's break both questions down properly.

## How Long Does an IV Take? Session Length Explained

From the moment your nurse starts your drip, most IV Salt Rejuvenation treatments take **45 to 60 minutes** to finish. Add a few minutes on the front end for your health review and IV placement, and a full mobile visit runs about **60 to 90 minutes** door to door, which is the range you will see on our [service menu](/#services).

A few things can change that:

- **Drip contents.** A straightforward hydration bag runs faster than a drip with multiple added vitamins, since some ingredients infuse at a slower, safer rate.
- **Your veins and hydration level.** Well hydrated veins take a drip more quickly. Ironically, the more dehydrated you are, the slower things can start.
- **Your comfort.** If you prefer a slower drip rate, your nurse can adjust. You are on your couch, not in a waiting room, so there is no rush.

Because we come to you, there is no drive, no lobby, and no sitting in a paper gown. Most clients work, watch TV, or nap through the whole thing.

## How Fast Does IV Therapy Kick In?

This is where IV therapy earns its reputation. Because fluids and nutrients go directly into your bloodstream, absorption starts immediately, with none of the 30 to 60 minute wait (or longer) that drinking fluids requires while your gut does its work. Many clients report feeling noticeably better before the bag is empty, especially when the problem is dehydration from heat, illness, a long night, or a hard workout.

## How Long Does IV Therapy Last in Your Body?

Here is the honest, no hype version.

**The fluids themselves** integrate into your body's fluid balance right away. If you were dehydrated, that deficit is corrected by the end of your session, which is why the turnaround can feel dramatic.

**The hydration benefit** typically carries most people for **a few days up to about a week**. How long depends on what your body is up against: summer heat, exercise, alcohol, illness, caffeine, and how much water you drink afterward all draw down the tank faster or slower.

**Added vitamins and electrolytes** are used or stored on their own timelines. Water soluble vitamins like B and C are put to work over the following days, and your body clears what it does not need. This is normal and expected.

One thing IV therapy is not: permanent. Think of it like a full tank of gas rather than a new engine. It restores you quickly and completely, then normal life starts drawing the level back down. That is also why hydrating well after your drip stretches your results.

## Does IV Therapy Work? An Honest Answer

Yes, and it is worth being precise about what "work" means, because this industry has plenty of overpromising and we would rather you trust us.

### What is well established

**Rapid rehydration is not controversial.** <a href="https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/21635-iv-fluids" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">IV fluids are how hospitals have treated dehydration</a> for the better part of a century, precisely because they work faster and more completely than drinking. The <a href="https://health.clevelandclinic.org/iv-vitamin-therapy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cleveland Clinic notes that IV delivery sends nutrients directly into your bloodstream</a>, bypassing the digestive system entirely, with nearly 100 percent of the dose absorbed. Compare that to oral fluids and supplements, where gut health, nausea, and metabolism all take a cut.

If your goal is [bouncing back from dehydration](/blog/iv-hydration-therapy-treasure-coast/) caused by heat, a stomach bug, travel, exercise, or a long night, IV hydration does exactly what it says.

### What is promising but less proven

The broader wellness claims around vitamin drips, things like sustained energy, immunity, and mental clarity, have real biological logic behind them (B vitamins and magnesium genuinely are involved in energy production) but limited high quality research in healthy people so far. Medical experts, including Cleveland Clinic physicians, note that studies on IV vitamins as a general wellness tool are still catching up to the trend.

We think you deserve to know that. Plenty of our clients feel a real boost from vitamin add-ons and book them again. We simply will not tell you a drip cures illness, replaces sleep, or substitutes for a decent diet, because it does not.

### What actually makes the difference

Who administers your drip matters as much as what is in it. Unregulated drip bars have drawn warnings from the FDA and medical boards. At IV Salt Rejuvenation, every treatment is administered by a licensed registered nurse under medical director supervision, with a health history review before anything is placed. That is the standard you should demand from any IV provider, including us.

## How Often Should You Get IV Therapy?

For most people, IV therapy is an as-needed tool rather than a schedule. Common patterns among our Treasure Coast clients:

- **As needed:** after illness, travel, a long weekend, or a brutal outdoor stretch. This is most of our bookings.
- **Around events:** before or after races, beach days, weddings, and other occasions where you want to feel your best.
- **Recurring wellness:** some clients like a monthly drip as part of their routine. Your nurse can talk through whether that makes sense for you.

If you find yourself needing frequent IVs just to feel normal, that is a conversation for your doctor, not a drip menu. Persistent fatigue or dehydration deserves a real medical workup.

## What Affects How Long Your Results Last

Want to stretch your drip further? The biggest factors are within your control:

- **Keep drinking water afterward.** A drip resets your baseline; water maintains it.
- **Mind the Florida heat.** Outdoor time in a Stuart or Port St. Lucie summer pulls fluids fast. Rehydrate as you go.
- **Go easy on alcohol and excess caffeine,** both of which speed fluid loss.
- **Match intake to activity.** Hard workouts and long days on the water raise your needs.
